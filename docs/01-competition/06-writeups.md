## What your Writeup must contain (from the Overview)

- ≤ **2,000 words**; **40-char commit SHA** at the top; GitHub repo under **Project Links**
- **Media Gallery cover image** + 3 figures: **architecture diagram**, **score-vs-cost plot**, **failure breakdown on the 29 dev tasks**
- Cover all 5 rubric criteria: Insight & Failure Analysis 25 · Engineering Reasoning 25 · Cross-Model Generalizability 15 · Efficiency & Scaling 20 · Clarity & Visuals 15
- Disclose the **model that generated `artifacts/`** and the **local judge model** if one was used
- Steps: Writeups tab → New Writeup → Project Links → Submit (you can un-submit, edit, and resubmit until **Jan 5, 2027 23:59 UTC**)
