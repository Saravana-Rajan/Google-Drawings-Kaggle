## Original post (summary)

- **First timers:** no question is too basic; start your own thread or ask here. New to Kaggle? Watch the intro videos on site etiquette, Kaggle lingo, and entering competitions with Kaggle Notebooks. Models can be published on Kaggle Models.
- **Looking for a team?** Use the *Team Up* feature.
- Follow the Kaggle community guidelines.
- **Official Discord:** https://discord.gg/kaggle
  1. Discord competition channels are **public**. Don't share private info; private code/data sharing outside your team is still not allowed, and code must be shared publicly via Kaggle forums/notebooks.
  2. Discord is **not monitored** by staff/hosts. Keep important questions, insights and writeups on the Kaggle forums.
